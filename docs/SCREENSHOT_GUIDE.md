# 📸 Lab Screenshot & Evidence Guide

To give your project 100% authenticity and prove to interviewers that you built and executed this yourself, take these **4 core screenshots** and save them in the matching folders:

---

### Screenshot 1: EDR Process Telemetry
* **Folder:** `docs/screenshots/edr-telemetry/01-limacharlie-process-tree.png`
* **What to capture:**
  In the LimaCharlie web dashboard, click on your Windows sensor, go to **Processes** or **Timeline**, and show the process tree where `powershell.exe` or `cmd.exe` launched `certutil.exe` or `rundll32.exe`.
* **Caption:** *LimaCharlie EDR live process lineage showing certutil.exe execution.*

---

### Screenshot 2: SOAR Story in Tines
* **Folder:** `docs/screenshots/soar-workflow/02-tines-soar-story.png`
* **What to capture:**
  In your Tines workspace, take a screenshot of your visual story flow showing the 4 connected blocks:
  `[Webhook Ingest] -> [Transform Payload] -> [VirusTotal API] -> [Send Slack Alert]`
* **Caption:** *Tines automated SOAR story orchestrating threat enrichment and notification.*

---

### Screenshot 3: Slack Alert Card
* **Folder:** `docs/screenshots/slack-alerts/03-slack-incident-card.png`
* **What to capture:**
  In your private Slack channel `#soc-alerts`, take a screenshot of the alert message card showing the host name, command line, VirusTotal detection score, and the **[ Isolate Machine ]** button.
* **Caption:** *Interactive Slack alert card generated by Tines SOAR.*

---

### Screenshot 4: Network Isolation Confirmation
* **Folder:** `docs/screenshots/containment/04-endpoint-isolated.png`
* **What to capture:**
  On the Windows VM, show a command prompt trying to `ping 8.8.8.8` (failing with "General failure" or timeout) while the LimaCharlie dashboard shows the sensor status as **"ISOLATED"**.
* **Caption:** *Successful automated network isolation executed via LimaCharlie API.*
